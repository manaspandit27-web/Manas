HOW THIS WORKS

This is the real site, exactly as it is served, inside a frame. Nothing you do here changes the site until Claude applies your edits.

  • Click any text to rewrite it. ⏎ keeps it, esc leaves it. The words change on the page at once, and wherever else those same words appear.
  • Buttons, links and boxes still work. To rewrite the words on one, hold ⌥ option and click it. The same for the grey hint inside an empty box.
  • Each rewrite can carry a note for Claude (“remove this whole box”, “say this only once”). A note on its own, with the words left alone, is kept too.
  • SAVE writes everything to text-edits/edits.json. Until you save, your rewrites are kept by this browser.
  • Move around the site with its own links and buttons; your rewrites follow you to every page. ALL EDITS lists them, with UNDO.
  • EDITING: OFF gives you the page exactly as a visitor has it.

When you are done, SAVE and tell Claude /text-edit apply: each edit is found in the source and changed there. Then reload this page to see the site as it now reads.
